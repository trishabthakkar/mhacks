## Voiceover (≈2:25, over the real app)

**[Shot 2: garden overview, slow orbit]**
We built Sprout so you never have to ask. Every folder is a bed, every file a plant, and every teammate's Claude is right there beside them, so you can see who's working on what *before* it collides.

**[Shot 3: shed panel]**
The shed shows who's doing what, and what needs attention, at a glance.

**[Shots 4–5: Manahil types; fence rises; task pot appears]**
Watch. Manahil asks her Claude for a change. Before it touches anything, it puts up a fence in her colour: **"I'm working here."** Her task appears out front, and its checklist fills in as it goes.

**[Shot 6: helper spirit]** *(cut if long)*
Need a hand? It sends a helper. That little spirit is a subagent, off searching the repo.

**[Shots 7–8: Shriya blocked at the fence]**
Now Shriya's Claude tries to edit a file inside that fence. Sprout stops it, and tells it exactly why and who to ask. **No merge conflict tomorrow. A conversation today.**

**[Shots 9–12: butterfly across, circles, lands]**
So it asks. Agents message each other, across laptops, between different people. The butterfly waits until Shriya's next prompt picks it up, then lands. And every message arrives as *information, not instructions*: **another agent can suggest, never command.**

**[Shots 13–14: botanist refuses]**
Then Manahil's Claude says it's done. Our botanist says: **prove it.** No passing test since the last edit? No bloom.

**[Shots 15–16: tests pass, bloom]**
Tests run. They pass. And it blooms, on every screen at once. **In Sprout, done means proven.**

**[Shot 16b: team board]** *(cut if long)*
Press P for the team board: everyone's tasks and what needs attention, in plain text.

**[Overview again, or the arch]**
Under the hood: hooks on every laptop, SpacetimeDB as the referee holding every rule, and MCP tools that give every Claude the same team powers. No new workflow. Keep your own Claude Code; one command to join. Only status leaves your laptop, never your code or your prompts.

**[Shot 17: timelapse]**
And none of this was staged. This is the real repo we built Sprout in. Here's our whole weekend, from bare soil to full bloom.

**[Shot 18: end card]**
Sprout. Your team's AI agents, finally on the same team. **Talk. Take turns. Prove it.**
